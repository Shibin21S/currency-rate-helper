What this project is, and what it's trying to do

Currency Rate Helper is a proof-of-concept AI learning project that compares estimated foreign currency exchange rates across Canada's five major banks — TD, CIBC, RBC, Scotiabank, and BMO — against the real Bank of Canada baseline rate. The goal is simple: help everyday banking customers get a quick, plain-language sense of which bank is likely offering the better deal when exchanging currency, without having to check five different bank websites by hand.
